and two lead-capture tools.

in app/globals.css), framer-motion, react-three, Vercel.

Authoritative documentation: README.md and the code itself. Routes and content are driven by
lib/ (site-config, guides, case-studies, homelab-data, service-pages, pc-pages) — not by this file.
